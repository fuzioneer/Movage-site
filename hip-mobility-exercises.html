Mobility guide

Hip mobility exercises for tight hips

Tight hips show up as a stiff squat, a short running stride or a niggling lower back. These 12 hip mobility exercises cover the hip flexors, both directions of hip rotation and the groin, with photos and video demos from the Movage app.

Do them after a short warm-up or at the end of a workout. Breathe slowly, ease in to a strong stretch but never pain, and spend a little longer on your tighter side.

Hip flexors

Long hours sitting shorten the muscles at the front of the hip. These open them back up.

1. Couch stretch

Back knee on a cushion against a wall or the front of a sofa, shin up against it. Front foot forward in a lunge. Squeeze the back glute and lift your chest. Ease in.

Hold: 60s each side

2. Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch

Half-kneel, back knee under hip. Tuck your pelvis, squeeze the back glute and shift forward slightly. Reach the same-side arm overhead for more.

Hold: 45s each side

3. Lizard lunge

From a lunge, place both hands inside your front foot and lower your back knee. Let your hips sink forward and down. To go deeper, lower onto your forearms. Keep breathing.

Hold: 60s each side

Hip rotation

Internal and external rotation let you squat deep, change direction and sit cross legged without the lower back taking over.

4. 90/90 switches

Sit with both knees bent at 90°, one leg in front, one to the side. Rotate both knees to the other side and back, staying tall.

Hold: 60s (video demo)

5. Pigeon

Front shin across your body, back leg long behind. Square your hips and fold forward over the front leg. Support with a cushion under the hip if needed.

Hold: 60s each side (video demo)

6. Hip circles on all fours

On hands and knees, lift one knee slightly and draw the biggest circle you can with it, forward, out to the side and back. Keep your back still and move only from the hip. Reverse the direction.

Hold: 45s each side (video demo)

7. Windscreen wipers

On your back, knees bent and feet wider than your hips. Let both knees drop slowly to one side, then the other. Shoulders stay down.

Hold: 45s

8. Lying figure four

Lie on your back, cross one ankle over the opposite knee and pull that thigh towards your chest. Keep your head and shoulders relaxed on the floor.

Hold: 60s each side

Groin and inner thigh

Tight adductors limit squat width and side to side movement.

9. Frog stretch

On forearms and knees, knees wide, shins parallel, feet in line with knees. Rock your hips back slowly and hold where you feel the inner thighs.

Hold: 60s

10. Cossack squats

Wide stance. Shift into one hip and squat low on that leg while the other stays straight, toes up. Switch sides slowly.

Hold: 45s (video demo)

11. Reclined butterfly

Lie on your back, put the soles of your feet together and let your knees fall open. Rest your hands on your belly and let gravity do the work. Pillows under the knees if it's too strong.

Hold: 60s

Squat depth

Put it all together and practise the position you want to own.

12. Deep squat hold

Feet shoulder-width, sink into the bottom of a squat with heels down. Elbows inside the knees, chest up. Hold onto something sturdy, like a door frame or table edge, if you need to.

Hold: 60s (video demo)

How to check your hip mobility

Three tests show where your hips are tight: hip internal rotation, the Thomas test for hip flexors and 90/90 hip rotation. Test, do these exercises for a few weeks, then retest.
