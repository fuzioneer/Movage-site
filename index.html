The mobility test

Two ways to test. Same score.

Both give you a full Movage score out of 100 across all six zones. Pick the one that fits the time you've got.

Quick test

6 tests · 8 min

One test for each zone: shoulder flexion, back scratch, seated thoracic rotation, standing toe touch, 90/90 hip rotation and knee to wall.

  • Still gives your overall score and every zone score
  • Short enough to do every couple of weeks
  • The easy way to see if your routines are working

Best for regular check-ins.

Full test

12 tests · 17 min

Every test, with left and right measured separately, including hip flexors, hamstrings, ankle bend and external rotation.

  • Spots differences between your left and right side
  • Shows exactly which muscle or joint is holding you back
  • Each test links to a routine that targets it

Best for your first test, and every few months.

Why two? A detailed test is great for finding the problem, but nobody wants to spend 20 minutes on it every fortnight. The quick test uses the most telling move from each zone, so you can keep checking in without it feeling like a chore, then do the full test now and then for the detail.

How do the AR Wireframe Mobility tests work?

You prop your phone up and move in front of the camera. Movage tracks a wireframe of your body on your phone and measures your range of motion. The video is processed on your phone and isn't recorded or uploaded.

Do I need any equipment?

No. A bit of floor space is enough. A few moves use a towel, a wall, a chair or a step, and you can skip any you can't do.

Is it medical advice?

No. Movage gives general mobility guidance, not medical advice. If you have an injury, pain or a medical condition, check with a physiotherapist or doctor first, and stop any move that hurts.
