Delete your account

You can delete your Movage Community account at any time.

Delete it in the app (quickest)

  1. Open Movage and go to the Community tab.
  2. Scroll to Your profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then tap it again to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Or ask us by email

Email support@movage.co.uk from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Movage account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What's deleted

  • Your sign-in details and email address
  • Your nickname and sharing settings
  • Your community memberships, and any community you created that has no other members
  • Your shared session times and mobility score

What isn't affected

  • Your history on your phone. Tests, sessions and routines stay on your phone. To remove them, use Settings, then Delete all data, or delete the app.
  • Strava. Activities already sent to Strava stay in your Strava account. Disconnect Strava in Settings, and delete activities in Strava if you want to.
  • The early access list. To leave it, use the unsubscribe link in any of our emails or email us.
